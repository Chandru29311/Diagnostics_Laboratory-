Patient Guidance

Sample Collection Information

Understanding pre-test preparation ensures analytical precision and eliminates the need for repeated specimen draws.

Fasting Guidelines

Certain biochemical parameters are directly altered by digestive processes.

  • Fasting Blood Glucose: Fast 8–10 hours overnight.
  • Lipid Profile: Fast 10–12 hours. Avoid fatty dinner.
  • Water Allowed: Plain drinking water is permitted and encouraged to facilitate venipuncture.

Medication Protocols

Routine prescription medications can impact metabolic and endocrine values.

  • Thyroid Medication: Take morning levothyroxine after sample collection.
  • Blood Pressure Drugs: Usually taken with small sips of water; consult doctor.
  • Inform Phlebotomist: Notify lab staff of any anticoagulants or supplements.

Urine & Sterile Collection

Clean-catch collection is critical to prevent epidermal bacterial contamination.

  • Mid-Stream Urine: Discard the initial stream, collect the mid-stream in sterile container.
  • Stool Samples: Use provided sterile collection spoon; avoid toilet water contact.
  • Timely Delivery: Deliver home-collected specimens to the lab within 1 hour.
