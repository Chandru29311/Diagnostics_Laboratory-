Clinical Accuracy

Pre-Test Preparation Guidelines

Pre-analytical conditions directly affect diagnostic accuracy. Follow these evidence-based laboratory instructions before your specimen collection.

Critical Requirement

Fasting Protocol (8–12 Hours)

Required for Lipid Profile, Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS), Insulin, and Comprehensive Metabolic Panels.

  • Plain water is allowed & recommended to keep your veins well-hydrated.
  • Strictly avoid tea, coffee, milk, juices, chewing gum, and sodas.
  • Do not smoke or consume alcohol 24 hours prior to blood collection.
  • Best scheduled between 07:00 AM – 09:30 AM.
Medication Safety

Medications & Supplements

Essential for Thyroid Assays (TSH, FT4), Therapeutic Drug Monitoring, and Vitamin Profiles.

  • Continue life-saving medications (e.g. blood pressure) with water unless instructed otherwise.
  • Thyroid medicine: Postpone morning dose until after the blood sample is collected.
  • Biotin supplements: Pause high-dose biotin 48 hours prior to hormone immunoassays.
  • Inform our phlebotomist of all ongoing prescriptions during sample intake.
Sterility Protocol

Urine & Specimen Hygiene

Applies to Urinalysis, Urine Culture, Semen Analysis, and Stool Occult Blood tests.

  • Use only sterile, sealed containers supplied by Apex Diagnostics.
  • Midstream Clean Catch: Discard initial urine stream and collect the middle volume without touching container rim.
  • Submit specimen to the lab within 1–2 hours or refrigerate at 2–8°C.
  • For 24-hour urine tests, request our specialized preservative container.
Physical Readiness

Hydration & Activity Levels

Directly impacts Complete Blood Count, Creatinine, Electrolytes, CPK & Muscle Enzymes.

  • Drink 1–2 glasses of drinking water 30 minutes before phlebotomy to ease venipuncture.
  • Avoid intense gym workouts, heavy weightlifting, and marathon training 24 hours prior.
  • Rest quietly for 10–15 minutes before blood pressure and blood drawing.
  • Wear loose-fitting clothing with sleeves that roll up easily past the elbow.
